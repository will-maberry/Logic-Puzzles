How to Play

Place one tent beside each tree, horizontally or vertically. Each tent belongs to one tree. Match the number of tents shown beside every row and column. Tents cannot touch, even at corners.

  • Left click: Place or remove a tent.
  • Trees: A tree is fixed and cannot be changed.
  • Right click and drag: Paint or clear X marks.
  • Difficulty: Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard.
  • New puzzle: Generate another board at the selected difficulty.
  • Save and load: Keep one puzzle in this browser for 30 minutes.
  • Reveal solution: End the game and show the solution
